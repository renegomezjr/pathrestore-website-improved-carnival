Meet Rene Gomez

A thoughtful conversation.
A practical next step.

Rene brings a practical, reflective, relational approach to mentoring and coaching. There’s room to talk honestly, notice patterns, and choose what you’re ready to work on—without having to arrive with everything sorted out.

He is a National Board Certified Health & Wellness Coach (NBC-HWC), trained at Mayo Clinic, with a Master of Divinity (MDiv) and more than 15 years of pastoral care and counseling experience. Today, his work at Path Restore is mentoring and coaching; he does not provide therapy or clinical mental health treatment.

His Christian background informs his care for people. Spiritual reflection is welcome when it’s helpful to you, whether faith is central to your life, a source of questions, or outside what you want to discuss.

How we work together

Start where you are.
Build from there.

  1. Start with a free conversation

    Share what brings you here, ask questions, and get a sense of whether working with Rene feels like a fit.

  2. Clarify what matters

    Look at where your time and energy go, what’s getting in the way, and what you want to give more attention to.

  3. Choose realistic next steps

    Decide on something you can practice in everyday life: a habit, a conversation, or a boundary.

  4. Reflect, adjust, and build sustainable change

    Talk through what you notice, learn from what didn’t work, and adjust the next step to fit your life.
