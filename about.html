Meet Rene Gomez

A human connection
comes first.

Rene is a National Board Certified Health & Wellness Coach (NBC-HWC), trained at Mayo Clinic, with a Master of Divinity (MDiv) and more than 15 years of pastoral care and counseling experience. His current work at Path Restore is mentoring and coaching. Path Restore does not provide counseling, therapy, or clinical mental health treatment.

Choosing someone to walk alongside you is personal. Rene brings a practical, reflective approach to goals and habits, relationships, marriage preparation, and questions of life direction.
